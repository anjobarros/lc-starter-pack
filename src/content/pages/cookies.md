## What Are Cookies

Cookies are small files stored on your device. Similar technologies include local storage and pixels.

## How We Use Them

- **Essential** — required for basic site function
- **Analytics** — help us understand traffic and usage
- **Preferences** — remember choices such as language or consent

## Your Choices

You can control cookies through your browser settings. Blocking some cookies may affect site features. Where required, we request consent before non-essential cookies.

## Third Parties

Some cookies may be set by third-party tools (analytics, embeds). Their policies apply to those cookies.
