## Commitment

We aim to make this website usable by as many people as possible, including people who use assistive technologies.

## Standards

We strive to conform to the Web Content Accessibility Guidelines (WCAG) 2.2 Level AA where feasible.

## Measures

- Semantic HTML and meaningful labels
- Keyboard-operable interactive elements
- Visible focus states
- Text alternatives for meaningful images
- Respect for `prefers-reduced-motion` where motion is used
